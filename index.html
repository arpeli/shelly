A small corner of forever

For Michelle — My Shelly

A place to keep the poems and letters I write for you, so our words can outlive every passing day.

Poems

When You Laugh

June 12

The room forgets its shadows
whenever you laugh—
like morning spilling gold
across a tired floor.

I keep that sound with me
in every hard hour,
a small, bright lantern
I never have to light twice.

Postcard Sky

May 28

Tonight the clouds looked hand-painted,
pink and quiet at the edges.
I almost called the sky by your name,
because beauty should answer to something.

Letters

Dear Shelly, On Ordinary Days

June 2

I love the dramatic moments, but I think I love our ordinary days even more. The grocery lists, the shared silence, the random song lyrics we repeat all week—those are the threads that make our life feel handmade.

Thank you for making even the smallest days feel chosen and warm. If forever is made of little moments, I want all of mine with you.

Dear Michelle, For the Future Us

May 16

One day we will read this back in a different home, older and kinder and still a little goofy. I hope that version of us remembers how fiercely this version believed in love.

If you ever doubt what you mean to me, come back here. Every word is proof: you are my favorite person, my peace, and my best decision.
