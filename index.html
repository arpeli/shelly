Letters for Shelly

A small forever-place for the words I write when my heart is full.

Letters

A Quiet Morning Note

March 8

Shelly, mornings feel softer when I imagine your laugh in the next room. Even the sunlight seems to wait for you before it touches anything. If love is a language, then you are my first fluent sentence.

After a Long Day

April 21

Today was loud and restless, but thinking of you made it gentle. You have a way of turning tired hours into something worth keeping. Thank you for being my calm place in every storm.

Sunday Promise

May 12

I promise to keep choosing you in the ordinary moments — in grocery lines, late buses, and rainy evenings. Great love is built there, and I want to build ours every single day.

Poems

Orbit

You are not the moon,
and still my tides obey.
Every road I take by chance
curves softly your way.

Postcard Heart

I would mail you every sunset
if envelopes could hold flame,
but all I have are simple words:
Shelly, and your name.

Home

Home is not a city,
not a street, not a view.
It is wherever my pulse says
"I am with you."
